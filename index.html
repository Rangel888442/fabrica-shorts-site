Fabrica Shorts

A private production tool for one YouTube channel.

What it is

Fabrica Shorts is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel UC02Wx_lbpGzrntYlMUMX8jw. It creates short animated videos (stick-figure animations about life lessons) entirely with code on the owner's own computer, and uploads them to the owner's channel.

How it uses the YouTube API

  • Upload: sends the finished video to the owner's channel as private, with its title, description, tags and thumbnail. The owner reviews it in YouTube Studio and decides whether to publish it.
  • Read: reads the owner's own channel information and public video statistics to check what works and improve future videos.
  • Analytics: reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, retention) for the same purpose.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's account.
